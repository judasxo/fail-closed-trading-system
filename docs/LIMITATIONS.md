# Limitations

This project deliberately does not claim:

- a profitable strategy;
- a repeatable source of alpha;
- live-money execution readiness;
- live slippage or fill quality from paper fills;
- long-term unattended reliability from one accepted 24-hour run;
- authoritative market data from agreement between redistributors;
- that two rejected hypotheses exhaust the strategy space; or
- that independent language-model review proves reviewer identity.

## Research limitations

The historical studies are tests of exact frozen translations, not universal statements about related anomalies. One HYP-0001 data-source amendment and evaluation step occurred without the same pre-result independent review applied to the core statistical design; the private audit trail records that governance deviation.

The quarter-end HYP-0001 value is descriptive and post-result. Its MDE calculation explains why a rescue would be weak; it is not itself a registered subgroup test.

## Operational limitations

The demonstrated mutation paths are paper-only. The public repository contains no broker client and cannot place an order. The crypto cost measurement is venue- and period-specific. Cross-venue readiness was not available on every observation.

The original project charter included a minimum-size live deployment that was not completed. Future scoping should separate controllable engineering work from external account prerequisites and approvals.

## Public-evidence limitation

`evidence/outcomes.json` is a sanitized derivative. Its source hashes bind it to private canonical artifacts, but those private files are not all distributed publicly because the release minimizes account and operational metadata. A reviewer of the complete private bundle can reproduce the deeper audit.
